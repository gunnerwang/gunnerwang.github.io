About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Production Engineering at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working with Prof. Xi Vincent Wang and Prof. Lihui Wang. I received my Zhi Yuan Honorary Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2025 and was a Visiting Scholar at KTH from 2023 to 2024.

My research spans LLM agents for manufacturing system design and governance, world models for human anticipation and adaptive collaboration, and physical AI for closed-loop robot control. I also study robust industrial tabular learning under temporal shift and human-centric digital twins for Industry 5.0.
