About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher specializing in Human-Robot Collaboration and Intelligent Manufacturing. Currently completing my Zhi Yuan Honorary Ph.D. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, I have had the privilege of conducting research as a Visiting Scholar at KTH Royal Institute of Technology under the supervision of Prof. Lihui Wang.

My research focuses on developing innovative AI-driven solutions for sustainable manufacturing, including vision-based human mesh recovery, multimodal action recognition, and digital twin systems for Industry 5.0. I am passionate about creating technologies that enhance human-robot collaboration while advancing manufacturing efficiency.
