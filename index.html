Representative work across intelligent systems, robot behavior, and human-centered production.

01 / Agentic manufacturing

LLM agents for manufacturing system design

From natural-language production requirements to geometry-feasible manufacturing layouts. Multiple LLM agents work with 3D machine knowledge and human feedback to support rapid reconfiguration.

CIRP Annals · 2026Read paper ↗

02 / Physical AI

Ghost Attractor Networks

A basin-structured dynamical decoder for multimodal, closed-loop sequential generation. Learned potential–drift dynamics provide a structured latent space for stable behavior and mode switching.

arXiv preprint · 2026Read preprint ↗

03 / Industrial machine learning

Learning under temporal shift

Multi-timescale representations and adaptive routing for industrial tabular data. This work addresses changing operating conditions and temporal drift in deployed predictive models.

Neural Networks · 2026Read paper ↗

04 / Human–robot collaboration

Human-centric digital twins

A framework for high-fidelity digital twins of collaborative work cells, combining human reconstruction, motion tracking, and procedure recognition to connect people, robots, and their shared workspace.

Journal of Manufacturing Systems · 2025Read paper ↗
