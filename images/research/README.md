# Research visuals

- `layouts.webp`: complete figure showing generated layouts and shopfloor configurations, from the author's CIRP Annals 2026 paper, “LLM agents-driven layout generation for reconfigurable manufacturing systems.” https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cirp.2026.03.015
- `ghost.webp`: complete mechanism figure from the author's public arXiv version of “Ghost Attractor Networks: Basin-Structured Dynamical Decoders for Closed-Loop Sequential Generation.” https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18315
- Both raster figures are resized and encoded for the web without removing panels or changing their contents.
- `temporal.svg` and `twin.svg`: original conceptual illustrations for this website, explicitly labeled as such. They do not reproduce experimental results or claim to be figures from the linked papers.
